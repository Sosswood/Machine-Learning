Comparison of Units 6 and 11

Comparison of Units 6 and 11 and the progress I made between the two.

Notes on Unit 6

Collaboration

    Unit 6 was a collaborative project with three other team members. We were able to work together to share resources and knowledge. Everyone made a signficicant contribution and our project was strengthened as a result.

Techniques

    The techniques required for the Unit 6 project were Data Preparation, Exploratory Data Analysis, Regression and Clustering. At the time, I thought these were quite complicated to write.

Notes on Unit 11

Collaboration

    Unit 11 was not a collaborative project. I had to stand on my own two feet. I found this challenging, but I love a challenge!

Techniques

    The techniques required for the Unit 11 project began with Data Preparation and Exploratory Data Analysis. Thanks to the work I'd done and the results shared by my colleagues in Unit 6, this part of the work was familiar by now.

    I then used Matrix Factorisation to build a Traditional Learning Model. I evaluated it using RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error), MAE (Mean Absolute Error) and NDCG@10 (Normalised Discounted Cumulative Gain at 10).

    I also looked at Explanability using SHAP.

    Next, I used Neural Collaborative Filtering to build a Deep Learning Model. I evaluated it using the same metrics as my traditional model so that I could compare the two.
