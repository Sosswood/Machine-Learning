      Reflections

      I was really worried about working as part of a group. Not knowing anybody on my course and not knowing whether my assigned team members would be knowledgeable or committed to the task was worrying.

      When I found out that we were based across three different countries and all worked full-time jobs with varying schedules, I just didn’t see how it could work.

      Then Adaeze and Damiano managed to find time to get together and prepare our team contract, when Jeroen and I simply did not have any availability in that short time frame. The contract was reviewed, agreed and signed by all of us. That was when I knew I was part of a great team who would pull together, support each other and take the initiative to ensure we hit those deadlines.

      The rest of the project followed this pattern. We met on Tuesday evenings, we shared out the work, we updated anyone who couldn’t make the meeting itself and we came up with a plan for each part of the work that would allow us to submit on time, with a little contingency in place for when life got in the way of deadlines.

      Everybody played to their strengths and used their skills to complete the project and we all pulled together to reduce our text to fit the word count – I think that may have been harder than the technical work.

      All in all, it was a very successful project and I was happy with the outcome.
